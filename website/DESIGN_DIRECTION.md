# EasyPOS landing page — Une journée plus simple

## Promise

EasyPOS makes the daily work of an independent shop easier: find a price, make a sale, know what is left, and keep clients and suppliers in view. It works for different kinds of merchandise. The first screen must say this before showing software.

## Art direction

An editorial portrait of a real shop day, mixed with precise printed retail details. Warm paper `#F6F3EB`, ink `#15211F`, deep teal `#125A55`, mint `#DDF1E7`, and a restrained brand yellow `#F0C84A`. Avoid the previous dark futuristic dashboard and floating product montage. Use generated, unbranded photography of an owner and team. Use actual EasyPOS Figma screen exports in the workflow. Thin shelf rules, receipt edges, price labels, and modest number markers guide the eye. Type is bold and direct, with spacious headings and compact operational details.

## Story

1. **Hero:** “Votre commerce. Une journée plus simple.” Owner with a phone and laptop in a mixed merchandise store. Free, open source, for everyday retail.
2. **Relief:** prices are in the product record, team members can find the right item, and each sale feeds the stock view. A simple interactive switch shows the shift from memory and scattered notes to a shared process.
3. **Setup:** one Windows laptop and local Wi-Fi; phone connects on the same network. Daily use does not need internet once installed. A scanner or printer is optional. Downloads and updates need internet.
4. **Real workflow:** add product with photo and price, find or scan it on Android, complete sale, review stock and alerts. Show Figma EasyPOS screens, labelled as design examples.
5. **Capabilities:** catalogue and barcodes; stock, inventory and low/out alerts; customers and suppliers; staff access, reports and backups.
6. **Value:** MIT open source, no software subscription, existing laptop and phone can be used. No invented financial savings number.
7. **Close:** download release, installation guide, and concise FAQ.

## Motion and access

Scroll reveals are subtle. A workflow tab changes the screenshot and explanatory text. Everything stays available with JavaScript disabled. Respect reduced motion, visible focus, descriptive image text, and minimum 44 px touch targets. Mobile stacks content and avoids horizontal overflow.

## Product accuracy

Windows x64 host, Android companion app with native barcode camera, same local Wi-Fi pairing. iPhone browser access has camera limits on local HTTP; do not promise native iPhone scanning. The system is free under MIT. The local server must remain running for phone access.
